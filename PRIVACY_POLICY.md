# Privacy Policy for DocuCapper

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2025  
**Last Updated:** November 26, 2025

## Introduction

DocuCapper ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Images**: When you capture or upload document images via the camera or gallery, these images are stored locally on your device.
- **Extracted Text**: Text extracted from document images via OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is stored locally on your device.
- **Notes and Annotations**: Any notes, titles, or summaries you create are stored locally on your device.
- **Device Permissions**: Camera and photo library access (to enable document capture).

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, app version (for crash reporting and app improvements).
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymous usage data (feature usage, session duration) may be collected to improve the app (optional, no third-party tracking).
- **Crash Logs**: Stack traces and error information (if crash reporting is enabled) to diagnose issues.

### 1.3 Optional AI Features

- **Summarization (if enabled)**: If you use the optional text summarization feature, extracted text may be sent to OpenAI's API to generate a summary. See Section 3 for details.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- **Local Storage & Display**: Images, extracted text, and notes are stored locally to enable document management, OCR, and note-taking features.
- **OCR Processing**: Text extraction is performed locally on your device using Google ML Kit; no data is sent to external servers for this feature.
- **Optional Summarization**: If you enable the summarization feature, extracted text is sent to OpenAI to generate summaries. See Section 3.
- **App Improvements**: Anonymous usage data helps us identify bugs, improve performance, and enhance features.
- **Legal Compliance**: We may use your information to comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests.

---

## 3. AI Summarization Feature

### 3.1 How It Works

The DocuCapper app includes an optional text summarization feature that uses OpenAI's API. When you request a summary:
- Extracted text is sent to OpenAI's servers for processing.
- OpenAI returns a summarized version of the text.
- The summary is displayed in the app and stored locally on your device.

### 3.2 Data Handling

- **Your Data**: Extracted text sent to OpenAI is subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/privacy
- **Encryption in Transit**: Data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted).
- **No Local Logging**: We do not log or store copies of text sent to OpenAI on our servers.
- **Opt-In**: The summarization feature is optional and disabled by default. You have full control over when to use it.

### 3.3 OpenAI's Privacy

By using the summarization feature, you also agree to OpenAI's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. OpenAI may retain data for a limited time as described in their policies. For details, visit: https://openai.com/privacy

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

### 4.1 Local Storage

- **Device-Only**: Images, extracted text, and notes are stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (SharedPreferences on Android).
- **No Cloud Backup**: By default, your data is not backed up to cloud servers (unless you enable device backup through your OS settings).
- **Encryption**: Local storage uses your device's encrypted file system.

### 4.2 Data Deletion

- You can delete individual notes, scan history entries, or images at any time within the app.
- Deleted data is removed from local storage and cannot be recovered by the app.
- If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is deleted.

### 4.3 Server Security

- DocuCapper does not operate backend servers to store your data.
- Communication with OpenAI (if using summarization) is encrypted and subject to OpenAI's security measures.

## 5. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

- **No Third-Party Sharing**: We do NOT share your images, extracted text, or notes with third parties (except OpenAI if you use summarization).
- **Legal Requests**: If legally required (e.g., subpoena, court order), we may disclose information as required by law.
- **Service Providers**: We do not use service providers to process your personal data beyond the optional OpenAI summarization service.

## 8. Your Rights & Controls

### 8.1 Access & Deletion

- You can access, view, or delete your data (images, notes, extracted text) within the app at any time.
- You can delete individual items or clear all data by uninstalling the app.

### 8.2 Device Permissions

- You can manage app permissions (camera, photo library) through your device's Settings.
- If you deny camera access, you will not be able to capture documents but can still import from gallery.

### 8.3 Opt-Out of Summarization

- The summarization feature is optional and can be disabled/not used.
- If you do not use the summarization feature, your data is never sent to external servers.
